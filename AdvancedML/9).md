### B) Causal Filter Convolution

Starting with the definition:
$$\begin{align}
y[n] = (x \star h)[n] &= \sum_{k=-\infty}^n x[k]h[n-k] \tag{}\\
&= \sum_{k'=0}^\infty x[n-k']h[k'] \\
&= \sum_{k'=0}^\infty h[k']x[n-k'] \\
&= \sum_{k'=0}^\infty \left(h[k']D^{k'}\right)x[n] \\ \\
&= h[0]x[n]+h[1]Dx[n]+h[2]D^2x[n]\dots
\end{align}$$
